# After launch

**Days 1–30:** verify enquiry acceptance, notifications and follow-up; review actual service/region demand; inspect indexing and broken links; complete operating evidence and obtain genuine approved project photographs.

**Days 31–60:** improve journeys based on enquiries; add permitted case studies and useful specialist-reviewed guidance; publish more service/location pages only where capability and distinct content justify them.

**Days 61–90:** assess recurring/multi-property demand and quote quality before expanding. Advertising, outreach, campaigns and analytics require owner authorization and fulfilment readiness.

Potential measurement: quote start, service selection, provider acceptance, verified lead receipt, call/email click. A click is not a completed call; acceptance is not inbox receipt. Never send personal identifiers or enquiry text to analytics. No fabricated targets.

Maintenance plan: periodic delivery verification, claims/coverage review, dependencies/host security, link checks and rollback rehearsal. This is a plan, not a configured automation or monitoring service.
