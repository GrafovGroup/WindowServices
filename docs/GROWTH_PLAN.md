# After launch

**Days 1–30:** verify enquiry acceptance, notifications and follow-up; review actual service/region demand; inspect indexing and broken links; complete operating evidence and obtain genuine approved project photographs.

**Days 31–60:** improve journeys based on enquiries; add permitted case studies and useful specialist-reviewed guidance; publish more service/location pages only where capability and distinct content justify them.

**Days 61–90:** assess recurring/multi-property demand and quote quality before expanding. Advertising, outreach and campaigns require owner authorization and fulfilment readiness. The owner has authorized optional Analytics, now deployed; see ANALYTICS.md.

Implemented measurement: page views, quote clicks/starts, validation/submission errors, provider acceptance and call/email clicks. Service and language are reporting dimensions. Inbox receipt is not measured. A click is not a completed call; acceptance is not inbox receipt. Never send personal identifiers or enquiry text to analytics. No fabricated targets.

Maintenance plan: periodic delivery verification, claims/coverage review, dependencies/host security, link checks and rollback rehearsal. This is a plan, not a configured automation or monitoring service.

Completed improvement phase: deeper bilingual service answers and business identity, mobile form/contact improvements, opt-in GA4, responsive-image optimization and cache-safe asset URLs. Google and Bing both read all 36 sitemap URLs. Next content priorities require the owner’s confirmed regions, genuine project evidence and approved review/profile links.
